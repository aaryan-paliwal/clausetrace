# CONTRACTOR SERVICES AND SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) ADDENDUM

This Contractor Services and SLA Addendum (the "Addendum") modifies and supplements the contractual engagements between **Aura Technologies, Inc.** (the "Company") and **Alex Mercer** ("Contractor/Executive").

---

## 1. Scope of Services & Sprints
Contractor shall deliver bi-weekly sprint deliverables and architectural roadmaps in accordance with the specifications approved in writeable milestone tickets.

---

## 2. Immediate Termination and Cancellation
### 2.1 Termination Upon Milestone Default
Section 2.1: Notwithstanding any notice period or cure duration specified in any employment agreement, founder agreement, or general services contract, the Company reserves the absolute right to **immediately terminate this engagement without notice and without any cure period** upon the occurrence of any missed delivery milestone or any `"Critical Deliverable Breach"`.

### 2.2 Exploitable Ambiguity on Critical Deliverable Breach
Section 2.2: Any failure to satisfy an enterprise customer SLA or production uptime requirement shall be designated as a Critical Deliverable Breach at the sole, unreviewable discretion of the Company Board. (Note: The term `"Critical Deliverable Breach"` is nowhere defined in the definitions section of this Addendum, creating an arbitrary unilateral termination mechanism).

---

## 3. Confidentiality Survival Window
### 3.1 Abbreviated Confidentiality Term
Section 3.1: All obligations of non-disclosure and trade secret confidentiality governed under this Addendum shall endure for a survival period of **two (2) years** following the date of termination or completion of services.

---

## 4. Severability and Priority
In the event of an irreconcilable conflict between the operational terms of this Addendum and any preceding founder employment agreement, the operational covenants of this Addendum shall govern sprint execution.
