# CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This Employee Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between **Aura Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and **Alex Mercer** ("Employee").

---

## 1. Confidential Information
Employee shall hold all Proprietary Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose any Proprietary Information to any third party without express written authorization of the Board of Directors.

---

## 2. Inventions and Side Projects
### 2.1 Side Project Carve-Out (Excluded Inventions)
Section 2.1: The Employee shall retain exclusive ownership of, and Company shall have no right or title to, any software, invention, or copyrightable work conceived, developed, or reduced to practice entirely during weekends or non-working hours, created exclusively on personal equipment and personal hardware, provided that such work does not directly relate to the Company's active commercial products or demonstrable pipeline.

### 2.2 List of Prior Inventions
All inventions developed prior to the effective date of this Agreement are set forth on Exhibit A and are excluded from the scope of Company assignment.

---

## 3. Assignment of Employment Inventions
Except as provided in Section 2.1, Employee hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to any and all Inventions made or conceived by Employee solely or jointly during the term of employment.

---

## 6. Comprehensive Intellectual Property Supremacy
### 6.4 Universal Assignment Override Clause
**Section 6.4 (Supremacy Over Inventions)**: Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, Section 2.1, or any prior understanding or written agreement to the contrary, the Company shall unconditionally own all right, title, and interest in and to any and all software code, intellectual property, machine learning weights, algorithms, and technical documentation authored, conceived, or reduced to practice by Employee at any time during Employee's tenure of employment with Company, regardless of whether created during working hours or on personal equipment, and Employee hereby grants a universal assignment of all such rights.

---

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the **State of California**, without giving effect to any choice of law principles that would require the application of the laws of a different jurisdiction.
