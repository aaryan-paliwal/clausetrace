# FOUNDER EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Founder Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2025, by and between **Aura Technologies, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and **Alex Mercer** ("Executive" or "Employee").

---

## 1. Position and Duties
The Company hereby employs Executive as Chief Executive Officer, reporting directly to the Board of Directors. Executive shall devote substantially all of Executive's business time, attention, and effort to the business and affairs of the Company.

---

## 2. Compensation and Benefits
### 2.1 Base Salary
The Company shall pay Executive an initial annual base salary of $185,000, payable in accordance with the standard payroll practices of the Company.

### 2.2 Annual Bonus
Executive shall be eligible to receive an annual discretionary target performance bonus of up to 35% of Base Salary, subject to milestones established by the Compensation Committee.

---

## 3. Confidential Information and Non-Competition
### 3.1 Confidentiality Survival
Executive agrees that all obligations of non-disclosure and confidentiality regarding Proprietary Information shall survive for a period of **five (5) years** following any termination of employment.

### 3.2 Non-Competition Scope
During the term of employment and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Executive shall not directly or indirectly engage in, perform services for, or invest in any business entity operating a direct competitor in generative AI policy compliance within North America.

---

## 4. Termination of Employment
### 4.1 Termination Without Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement without Cause upon delivering **thirty (30) days prior written notice** to the other party.

### 4.2 Termination For Cause and Cure Period
The Company may terminate Executive's employment for Cause; provided, however, that the Company must provide written specification of the alleged breach, and Executive shall have a **fifteen (15) day period to cure** such breach prior to the effective date of termination.

### 4.3 Severance Upon Involuntary Termination
Upon termination without Cause by the Company, Executive shall be entitled to receive six (6) months of base salary continuation and acceleration of twenty-five percent (25%) of unvested equity.

---

## 5. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement, and all claims or causes of action based upon, arising out of, or related to this Agreement, shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the **State of Delaware**, without regard to conflict of laws principles.
