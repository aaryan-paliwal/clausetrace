## 4. Payment Deadlines, Deferred Plans, and Late Penalty Fees

### 4.1 Payment Deadlines
All semester tuition, institutional fees, and housing charges must be paid in full by 5:00 PM on the designated Fee Payment Deadline (ordinarily the Friday preceding the first day of instructional classes).

### 4.2 Installment Payment Plans
Students experiencing temporary liquidity constraints may enroll in the Western Oak Four-Pay Installment Plan:
- Enrollment Fee: $50.00 per semester (non-refundable).
- Installment 1: 25% due by term start date.
- Installment 2: 25% due end of Week 4.
- Installment 3: 25% due end of Week 8.
- Installment 4: 25% due end of Week 12.

### 4.3 Late Payment Penalties and Administrative Holds
Failure to settle account balances by the published payment deadline triggers the following punitive actions:
- **Late Payment Penalty:** A late charge of $100.00 is assessed immediately upon the close of business on the payment due date. An additional 1.5% monthly compound interest is charged on outstanding balances thereafter.
- **Bursar Registration Hold:** Students with an outstanding delinquent balance exceeding $200.00 are placed on financial hold, barring course registration, transcript generation, diploma release, and campus housing renewal.

## 5. Institutional Course Drop, Withdrawal, and Refund Schedule

When a student officially drops a course or withdraws completely from Western Oak University, tuition charges are adjusted strictly in accordance with the institutional refund timetable. 

All refund calculations are determined by the formal timestamp of withdrawal registration through the University Registrar portal. Ancillary laboratory fees, technology fees, and late penalties are non-refundable after the first day of the instructional term.

### 5.1 Official Institutional Tuition Refund Table

| Academic Withdrawal Window | Calendar Timing / Instructional Progression | Tuition Refund Percentage |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| Full Refund Window | Prior to Day 1 through End of Week 1 (Census) | 100% Refund |
| Early Withdrawal Window | Week 2 of Instructional Term (Days 8 – 14) | 80% Refund |
| Mid-Term Withdrawal Window 1 | Week 3 of Instructional Term (Days 15 – 21) | 70% Refund |
| Mid-Term Withdrawal Window 2 | Week 4 of Instructional Term (Days 22 – 28) | 50% Refund |
| Late Withdrawal Window | Week 5 of Instructional Term through Term Conclusion | 0% Refund (No Refund) |

### 5.2 Specific Policy on Week-4 Term Withdrawals
As codified in Table 5.1 above, any student who officially withdraws from a course or from the university during the fourth week of the semester (instructional calendar days 22 through 28) is entitled to an institutional tuition refund of exactly 50%. The refund will be credited directly to the student's bursar account or returned via electronic direct deposit within fourteen (14) business days of the processed withdrawal notice.

## 6. Housing and Meal Plan Cancellation Penalties

Residence hall housing agreements and residential meal plans represent binding annual contracts. Cancellations submitted after the first week of classes are subject to a flat $500.00 contract termination penalty. Unused dining plan balances are prorated on a weekly schedule through Week 6, after which dining credits become entirely non-refundable.
