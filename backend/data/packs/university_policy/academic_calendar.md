# Western Oak University — Official Academic Calendar & Institutional Deadlines

**Academic Year:** 2026–2027  
**Issuing Authority:** Office of the University Registrar  
**Approval Body:** University Senate Executive Council  
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2026

---

## 1. Governance and Calendar Architecture

The Western Oak University Academic Calendar codifies statutory registration dates, instructional commencement, reading periods, examination blocks, fee deadlines, and degree conferral schedules. The academic cycle operates under a standard semester model comprising Autumn (15 instructional weeks), Spring (15 instructional weeks), and an accelerated Summer term (10 weeks across two sequential 5-week sessions).

All deadlines are strictly enforced at 11:59 PM Eastern Standard Time on the designated calendar date unless an earlier hour is explicitly cited in university regulations.

---

## 2. Autumn Semester 2026 Comprehensive Schedule

### 2.1 Pre-Semester Preparation & Registration
- **August 1, 2026:** Continuing student registration reopen period.
- **August 17, 2026:** Residence halls open for new first-year undergraduate matriculants.
- **August 20, 2026:** New Student Convocation and Academic Orientation.
- **August 21, 2026 (5:00 PM):** Autumn Semester Fee Payment Deadline. Accounts with unsettled tuition balances are assessed the statutory $100.00 late charge.

### 2.2 Instructional Progression & Term Deadlines
- **August 24, 2026:** Formal commencement of Autumn semester instructional classes.
- **August 31, 2026 (11:59 PM):** Official Add/Drop Deadline and Census Date. Final day for 100% tuition refund upon course drop. Last day to add semester courses without instructor approval.
- **September 7, 2026:** Labor Day Observance (University Closed; No Classes).
- **September 18, 2026 (11:59 PM):** End of Week 4 Course Withdrawal Window.
- **October 9, 2026:** Midterm Evaluation Reports posted by faculty in the University Student Portal.
- **October 15–16, 2026:** Autumn Reading Days (No Instructional Classes; Administrative Offices Open).
- **November 6, 2026 (5:00 PM):** Final Course Withdrawal Deadline. Last day to withdraw from an individual course with an official grade of 'W'. No course withdrawals permitted after this date.
- **November 25–29, 2026:** Thanksgiving Recess (University Closed).
- **December 4, 2026:** Final day of instructional classroom lectures.
- **December 5–6, 2026:** Designated Study and Reading Days.
- **December 7–12, 2026:** Autumn Final Examination Period.
- **December 15, 2026 (5:00 PM):** Final Faculty Grade Submission Deadline.
- **December 18, 2026:** Official Autumn Degree Conferral Date and Commencement Ceremony.

## 3. Spring Semester 2027 Comprehensive Schedule

### 3.1 Pre-Semester Milestones
- **January 4, 2027:** University administrative offices reopen following winter recess.
- **January 8, 2027 (5:00 PM):** Spring Semester Fee Payment Deadline. Late payment penalties assessed after 5:00 PM.
- **January 10, 2027:** Residence halls open for returning and incoming transfer students.

### 3.2 Instructional Term Progression
- **January 11, 2027:** Commencement of Spring semester instructional classes.
- **January 18, 2027:** Martin Luther King Jr. Holiday (University Closed; No Classes).
- **January 19, 2027 (11:59 PM):** Spring Add/Drop Deadline and Official Census Date.
- **February 5, 2027 (11:59 PM):** End of Week 4 Spring Course Withdrawal Window.
- **March 1–5, 2027:** Spring Recess (No Classes).
- **March 12, 2027:** Midterm Evaluation Reports published.
- **March 26, 2027 (5:00 PM):** Final Course Withdrawal Deadline ('W' grade).
- **April 23, 2027:** Final day of Spring instructional classroom lectures.
- **April 24–25, 2027:** Reading and Study Days.
- **April 26 – May 1, 2027:** Spring Final Examination Period.
- **May 4, 2027 (5:00 PM):** Faculty Final Grade Submission Deadline.
- **May 8, 2027:** Annual Baccalaureate Commencement and Master's Hooding Ceremony.

## 4. Summer Session 2027 Condensed Term Schedule

- **Session I (5-Week Block):** May 24, 2027 – June 25, 2027.
- **Session II (5-Week Block):** June 28, 2027 – July 30, 2027.
- **Full Summer 10-Week Term:** May 24, 2027 – July 30, 2027.
- **Summer Add/Drop Deadline:** Third day of instruction for respective session.
- **Summer Graduation Conferral:** August 13, 2027.
